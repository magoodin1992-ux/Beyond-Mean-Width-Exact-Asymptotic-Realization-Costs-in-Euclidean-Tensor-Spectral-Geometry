# Beyond Mean Width

**Exact Asymptotic Realization Costs in Euclidean Tensor Spectral Geometry**

Research preprint · November 25, 2026 · Version 1.0

[Read the full manuscript and proofs](BEYOND_MEAN_WIDTH.md)

The work extends the study of Euclidean tensor spectral geometry to exact asymptotic purchased wire costs in a specified coherent-label tensor restriction model.

## Main results

- Exact terminal-cut rate over arbitrary coefficient fields, using the established all-terminal graph-tensor theorem and a constructive extension to designated terminals.
- Exact asymptotic wire-cost linear program, with a polynomial-size root-to-terminal flow formulation.
- Euclidean auxiliary-site elimination by splitting-off and the triangle inequality.
- Gaussian mean-width dual and a necessary and sufficient geometric tightness criterion.
- Strict mean-width gaps for simplices with at least four vertices and generic interior receivers.
- Task-conditioned information pricing through contextual hypergraph coloring and asymptotic block compression.

For a unit equilateral triangle, requiring its centroid as an additional receiver leaves the spectral price unchanged but raises the optimal wire cost per bit from 3/2 to 1 + 1/√3, even when arbitrary finite Euclidean auxiliary sites are allowed.
